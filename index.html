Australia's Wildlife Crisis

The Silent Invasion

Since 1788, humans have unleashed hundreds of foreign species across Australia. The damage is greater than most Australians will ever know.

Part 1

A Paradise with a Problem

Australia is one of the most biologically extraordinary places on Earth. Around 80% of its mammals, reptiles, and flowering plants exist nowhere else in the world.

Yet today, Australia holds a record no country wants — the worst modern mammal extinction rate on the planet.
